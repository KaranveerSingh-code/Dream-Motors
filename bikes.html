Bikes

Top 4 bikes in my opinion.

  1. Kawasaki Ninja H2R
    This is an absolute engineering monster built strictly for closed-course track racing. It is famously powered by a 998cc supercharged inline-four engine that puts out a staggering 310 to 326 horsepower. Instead of mirrors, it features distinct carbon-fiber aerodynamic wings on the front fairing to generate extreme high-speed downforce.


  2. Ducati Panigale V4 Tricolore
    Combines massive 1,103cc V4 power with extreme collector rarity and a stunning Italian paint scheme.


  3. Ducati Panigale V4 R
    A street-legal World Superbike racing platform boasting a 998cc engine built to rev past 16,000 RPM.


  4. Kawasaki Ninja 300
    A lightweight, two-cylinder machine designed for accessibility rather than setting track records.
